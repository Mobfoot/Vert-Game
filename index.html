  • UP DOWNUp while rising, down while dropping, to pump speed.

  • MSound: music and effects, effects only, or off.

Rock the pad up and down with the skater to build speed. Once the speed bar passes the white air line you leave the lip. What you hold when you reach the lip decides the move: A for a handplant, left or right for a kick turn, nothing for a launch. In the air, hold A to grab the board. Spin or flip before or after the grab, and let go before you land. Stopped on the flat? Hold left or right to push off.
